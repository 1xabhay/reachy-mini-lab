    Recordings

      Calibration sweep

      Sweeps each axis and compares commanded pose against feedback. Reports gain, offset, RMS error and backlash. Moves the robot.

      Range of motion

      Walks each axis out to its documented limit (roll/pitch ±40°, yaw ±65°) and reports the span actually reached. Stops a direction as soon as the head stops making progress, so a blocked axis is not driven into its stop. Moves the robot.

      Rotation validation

      Rotates by a known angle and measures the rotation the camera actually saw, via ORB features and a RANSAC homography. Moves the robot.

      Visual scale calibration

      Fits pixels-per-degree across several angles to check the published camera intrinsics against reality. Moves the robot.
